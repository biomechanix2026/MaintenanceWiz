# Initial Concept
An autonomous, context-aware Industrial Decision-Support agent for a heavy steel manufacturing plant. It consolidates live sensors, manuals, delay logs, failure-analysis records, and ERP spares inventory into actionable maintenance decisions.

# Target Audience
- **Maintenance Engineer:** Diagnoses faults and validates agent-prepared work orders.
- **Reliability Manager:** Monitors overall plant health and asset degradation trends.
- **Plant Supervisor:** Oversees daily operations and reviews pre-shift autonomous reports.

# Primary Value Proposition
**Reduce Unplanned Downtime:** The primary success metric is minimizing unexpected equipment failures by leveraging Remaining Useful Life (RUL) predictions and early abnormality detection.

# Future Expansion
- **Real-time IoT Integration:** Transitioning from static/mock CSV data to live streaming sensor telemetry.
- **Automated ERP Ordering:** Empowering the agent to autonomously requisition required parts directly from the ERP system based on lead times and stock levels.

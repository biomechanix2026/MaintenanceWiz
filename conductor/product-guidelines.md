# Product Guidelines

## 1. Design & Branding
- **Theme:** Industrial, high-contrast. Use clear visual cues for statuses (e.g., Red for CRITICAL, Yellow for WARNING, Green for NORMAL).
- **Layout:** Dense but scannable. Prioritize data visibility and the "five-block" output format for quick consumption.
- **Typography:** Sans-serif, highly legible fonts suitable for dashboards.

## 2. Prose & Tone
- **Tone:** Professional, objective, and authoritative. Avoid conversational filler in the UI.
- **Clarity:** Use precise engineering and maintenance terminology (e.g., "Remaining Useful Life", "Spares Lead Time").
- **Action-Oriented:** Emphasize actionable recommendations and clear next steps over raw data presentation.

## 3. UX Principles
- **Efficiency:** The system should provide a unified "Consolidated Brain" view to reduce cognitive load and screen-switching.
- **Traceability:** Every decision or recommendation must be clearly linked to its source data, SOP, or logic (explainability via SHAP).
- **Compliance Enforcement:** Actions like closing a job must be gated until all safety and compliance steps are validated and logged.
